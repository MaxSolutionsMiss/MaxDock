Sending this back

Two minutes, and then you are done.
  1. Press “Save filled pack” at the bottom of this page. Your browser will download a small file called something like maxdock-onboarding-guelph.json. It usually lands in your Downloads folder.
  2. Reply to the email this came from and attach that file. That is all we need.
If your email will not let you attach it, some company systems block unfamiliar file types. Press Copy instead at the bottom, then paste into the body of your reply. It is the same information and it works just as well.

Printing gives you a paper copy to check or get signed off, but a printed or PDF copy cannot be loaded into MaxDock — somebody would have to type it all in again. Send the saved file, and print as well if it is useful to you.
